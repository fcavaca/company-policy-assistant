# Company Policy Assistant Assignment
Build a bot that answers policy questions and identifies the relevant policy.

## Required outcomes

- A deployed website comparing rules-based search, an LLM without a vector index, and an LLM with a vector index.
- The comparison shows each approach's answer, relevant policy, response time, token use, and tendency to provide answers unsupported by the policy database.
- Exactly two paragraphs explaining the comparison and preferred approach. A maximum of two tables may be included.
- A Slack bot that answers policy questions and identifies the relevant policy.
- A separate Slack workspace and testing channel that identify the student.
- An invitation to raz@sdu.dk to join the channel.

The Slack bot may run locally. Permanent hosting is not required.

## Deliverables

- Link to the deployed website
- Two-paragraph comparison
- Link to the source-code repository
- Screenshot showing the bot being called and answering a policy question in a channel
